# Interference in Thin Films and Transfer Matrix Formalism

**Course:** Intermediate Electromagnetism and the Quantum Physics of Radiation  
**Year:** 2024

A theoretical study of thin-film interference, including the conditions for constructive and destructive interference, and the application of transfer matrix formalism to multilayer optical systems.

The report also explores transmission resonances, partial coherence, and applications such as anti-reflective coatings and Bragg reflectors.
